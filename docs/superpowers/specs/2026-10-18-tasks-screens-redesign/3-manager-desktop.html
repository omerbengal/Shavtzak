יעל מזרחי—
  • Same calendar as on the phone, with both days side by side because the screen is wide. The clicked stage is outlined and stays in view.
  • The stage opens on the left instead of a sheet: details, contacts and all of its tasks as a table, sorted by report time, with untimed ones last.
  • Fixes happen in place: edit or delete the stage at the top, "משימה חדשה" for a task, and each row's pencil and bin appear on hover.
  • The "לבדיקה" row appears only when something needs fixing: a task whose assignee was taken off the event. Clicking it lists those tasks and outlines their stages.
  • That assignee shows struck through in red on the task's row. Stages without tasks (like lunch) are normal and carry no warning.
  • On a phone the same screen falls back to the calendar and the low sheet.
