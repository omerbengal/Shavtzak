  • Opens on the current stage: your open tasks there first, the next stage right under it as a glance, earlier stages folded above.
  • The report time is the timeline's axis (right column). One tap ticks a task or a subtask.
  • A late task of yours from an earlier stage stays pinned on top in red until it's ticked.
  • Every stage shows, even one with nothing of yours in it (like lunch), so the timeline is also the event's schedule.
  • No place per task (your call): the place shows once, on the stage.
  • A task with no report time sits at the end of its stage, marked "ללא שעה". It turns late only once its day is over.
