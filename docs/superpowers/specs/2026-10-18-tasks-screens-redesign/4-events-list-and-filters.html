  • The filters sit on their own row under the progress, marked with the filter icon and "סינון:", drawn as toggles. A switched-on one fills in and gets a tick.
  • Switching one on lists its tasks from every stage in the low sheet and outlines those stages; switching it off (or closing the sheet) clears it.
