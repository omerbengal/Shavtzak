העתק טקסט

Most reliable technically and easy to paste into WhatsApp. It is not an image, so it does not satisfy the screenshot-style request exactly.

My recommendation: B as the primary path, with C as fallback. The current dialog can remain detailed and scrollable; the share action creates a compact, RTL, WhatsApp-ready image.
